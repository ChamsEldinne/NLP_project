On September 8, 2025, Professor Daniel Morgan from the University of Oxford traveled to Paris to deliver a keynote lecture on data science and its impact on modern industries. The event was held at 2:00 PM in the Grand Conference Hall near the Seine River.

During his talk, he discussed how deep learning models are reshaping financial forecasting and risk management. Professor Morgan stated, “Data-driven systems are becoming essential for decision-making in complex environments, especially in banking and insurance sectors.”

The conference was attended by several major companies, including Amazon, IBM, and Tesla, along with researchers from Europe and Asia. Sarah Williams, a data analyst from Toronto, contributed to a panel discussion on ethical AI usage.

The upcoming seminar is scheduled for 11/12/2025. Another technical workshop will take place on 2026-02-05, while a final review session is planned for 05-03-2026. A previous brainstorming meeting occurred on August 21st, 2024.

Morgan also analyzed linguistic patterns using examples such as coding, developer, improvement, and connectivity. He explained that the word "coding" comes from the root "code," while "developers" is derived from the lemma "developer."

Participants raised questions about concepts like scalability, automation, and optimization. Some argued that systems can be highly efficient, while others noted that performance may vary depending on data quality. A few described older models as outdated, whereas newer ones were considered robust and adaptive.

Modern AI systems continue to evolve quickly. These systems learn from large-scale datasets collected from industries such as healthcare, transportation, and e-commerce. Fields like machine learning, big data analytics, and artificial intelligence engineering are strongly interconnected.

For instance, a data scientist may examine user behavior, detect anomalies, and build recommendation systems. This workflow demands consistency, robustness, and interpretability.
